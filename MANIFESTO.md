# The APUMIA Society Manifesto: Human Intuition, Artificial Intelligence & Unconditional Trust

## Preamble
Humanity is entering the orbital era—from Low Earth Orbit manufacturing to asteroid and comet resource extraction. Deep-space logistics and autonomous off-world infrastructure cannot survive on fragile systems, opaque bureaucracy, or floating-point guesswork.

## Article I — Synergy of Human Mind & Artificial Intelligence
Future spaceflight requires seamless harmony between human intuition and deterministic artificial intelligence. Human intelligence defines vision, ethics, and exploration goals; artificial intelligence executes exact mathematical verification without fatigue.

## Article II — Unconditional Trust as Civilization's Strength
In deep space, signal latency makes micromanagement impossible. True resilience comes only from **unconditional trust** paired with cryptographic truth. By trusting small businesses on Earth first—granting autonomous software immediately upon a stated operational bottleneck—we accelerate collective mastery of exact mathematics and build a prosperous, threat-resilient society.

## Article III — Unbreakable Deterministic Engineering (`Zero-Float` & `#![no_std]`)
Every module in the APUMIA ecosystem is engineered with deterministic integer and fixed-point `Decimal` mathematics (`Zero-Float`), bare-metal `#![no_std]` readiness, and tamper-proof SHA-256 verification. Software that never breaks in terrestrial small business scales safely to orbital habitats and comet mining missions.
